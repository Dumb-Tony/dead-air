# Campaign pacing — target, measurement and remaining gate

The intended experience is a 120–180 minute first-time campaign: fast combat, exploratory navigation, readable physical clues and room to absorb the story. This duration is **not verified for v0.10.0**.

## Content now present

Fourteen chapters, 524 finite starting infected, 91 discoverable field records/ciphers, fourteen supply-cache puzzles, seven optional personal keepsakes, twelve objective fixtures per new chapter, two coupled-circuit puzzles, illustrated arrivals, an opening cinematic and a conclusive three-page epilogue. The new nine-room maps use a 50 × 43 grid; the earlier layouts remain unchanged.

## Design budgets, not measured estimates

| Segment | Desired first-time pace | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chapters 1–4 | 25–35 minutes | Learn movement, machinery, the voice mystery and Mara’s real message |
| Chapters 5–7 | 20–30 minutes | Freight/clinic/coast rescue, expanded weapons and infected |
| Chapters 8–11 | 40–60 minutes | Follow independent evidence, meet Rook, secure the crossing |
| Chapters 12–14 | 30–45 minutes | Recover identities, preserve the case, complete physical isolation |
| Optional lore, arrivals and ending | 5–10 minutes | Personal context and emotional breathing room |
| **Total budget** | **120–180 minutes** | **Target awaiting first-time human validation** |

These are production budgets. They do not establish that each current chapter fills the allotted time. A player who already knows the game can finish much faster.

## What has actually been measured

The automated replay uses ordinary movement, interactions, combat, finite ammunition and healing. It knows every route and puzzle answer and reads instantly. All fourteen chapters plus supply caches and new keepsakes take about **35 minutes of simulated active time**. Maps, journals and cinematic pauses are excluded from the game's active-time counter. These tests demonstrate route viability and catch softlocks; they cannot establish a first-time completion time.

## Gate before advertising a two-hour campaign

Measure at least three first-time playthroughs without solutions supplied. Record wall-clock time minus breaks, active gameplay time per chapter, reading time, deaths/checkpoint repeats, missed optional content and points where a player needs an external hint. A useful result is a 120–180 minute typical run without repeated confusion or forced waiting.

If the median is below 120 minutes, expand purposeful routes and encounter/puzzle variety in the short chapters. Do not increase existing timers, slow movement, require all collectibles, add infinitely respawning enemies, or force repeated runs over cleared corridors merely to reach the duration target. If the median exceeds 180 minutes because of confusion, improve clue placement and navigation before cutting story.

The final narrative resolution is implemented; further pacing work should preserve its outcome rather than append another surprise transmitter after the ending.
