# Dead Air — narrative guide

## Premise

Alex Vale is Relay Station 6's overnight emergency technician. A distress call in the voice of Alex's sister Mara, an evacuation worker, brings Alex back after quarantine has failed. Alex enters through maintenance, finds the security post abandoned, and takes its emergency pistol. The lift shaft is flooded, forcing a journey through the station's machinery.

The player knows why they arrived, who they are looking for, and why they must repair the station before leaving. The PA then speaks in Alex's voice: “Do not let me reach the transmitter.”

## Chapter revelations — spoilers

1. **West Pump — the impossible call.** Mara's written instructions teach the route and the importance of heavy doors. A quarantine notice warns against familiar voices. The recovered evacuation manifest proves Mara left at 02:17, before the 02:43 distress call. The route continues through ventilation.
2. **Ventilation Works — the mechanism.** ECHO is an emergency system that reconstructs trusted voices from archived calls and contacts. Its carrier drives exposed dead personnel toward active equipment. Restoring the station is expanding the system's reach. The distress call was generated, not live.
3. **Power Exchange — the real message.** Mara's local, unaltered recording confirms she evacuated the civilians and is waiting above ground. She warns Alex not to trust another call in her voice. ECHO survives mains shutdown through emergency feeds; all feeds must be isolated before its carrier imprint can be erased.
4. **Transmission Chamber — the choice to finish.** Alex isolates three feeds, erases the carrier, and escapes through the loading gate. A source fragment reveals that the opening warning was assembled from Alex's old maintenance calls. ECHO's imitation has no supernatural knowledge. At the surface Alex reunites with Mara, and the carrier's victims stop moving.

## Delivery

- Three skippable opening cards establish the call, arrival and first objective. They use text rather than recorded voice acting.
- Three optional records per chapter supply physical evidence, instructions and character context.
- Chapter completion screens deliver the essential revelations even if records are missed.
- PA captions announce objective activity; they are intentionally unreliable in the fiction. Written records carry the verifiable evidence.
- The campaign ends conclusively after extraction. There is no implied playable fifth chapter.

## Continuity rules

Mara's genuine messages are written or stored on a local recorder. The misleading distress call is ECHO's reconstruction. Alex is a trained station technician, explaining machinery interactions; the pistol comes from the emergency security post. The zombies are established as carrier-exposed personnel, not a separate unrelated outbreak. Keep future expansions consistent with these facts, or explicitly revise this guide and the in-game records together.
