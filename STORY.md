# Dead Air — narrative guide

## Premise

Alex Vale is Relay Station 6's overnight emergency technician. A distress call in the voice of Alex's sister Mara, an evacuation worker, brings Alex back after quarantine has failed. Alex enters through maintenance, finds the security post abandoned, and takes its emergency pistol. The lift shaft is flooded, forcing a journey through the station's machinery.

The player knows why they arrived, who they are looking for, and why they must repair the station before leaving. The PA then speaks in Alex's voice: “Do not let me reach the transmitter.”

## Chapter revelations — spoilers

1. **West Pump — the impossible call.** Mara's written instructions teach the route and the importance of heavy doors. A quarantine notice warns against familiar voices. The recovered evacuation manifest proves Mara left at 02:17, before the 02:43 distress call. The route continues through ventilation.
2. **Ventilation Works — the mechanism.** ECHO is an emergency system that reconstructs trusted voices from archived calls and contacts. Its carrier drives exposed dead personnel toward active equipment. Restoring the station is expanding the system's reach. The distress call was generated, not live.
3. **Power Exchange — the real message.** Mara's local, unaltered recording confirms she evacuated the civilians and is waiting above ground. She warns Alex not to trust another call in her voice. ECHO survives mains shutdown through emergency feeds; all feeds must be isolated before its carrier imprint can be erased.
4. **Transmission Chamber — the choice to finish.** Alex isolates three feeds, erases the carrier, and escapes through the loading gate. A source fragment reveals that the opening warning was assembled from Alex's old maintenance calls. ECHO's imitation has no supernatural knowledge. At the surface Alex reunites with Mara, and the carrier's victims stop moving.
5. **Northline Freight — the second address.** Mara's paper manifest proves that a separate ECHO relay was shipped before quarantine. Relay 6 stays silent; its erasure succeeded. Alex restores rail points and the departure system to reach the clinic while Mara takes a staff walkway toward trapped patients.
6. **Salt Ward — twelve names.** Mara reaches twelve patients behind quarantine shutters. Alex restores emergency power and oxygen ventilation, opening the ambulance route. A local recording identifies Breakwater's independent coastal array as the remaining source.
7. **Breakwater Station — the coast is saved.** Alex cuts the physical uplink. Mara and all twelve patients survive. A collapsed inland road prevents reaching the hospital; a civil-defence warning predates the disaster. The local rescue remains successful.
8. **Civic Underpass — the signature.** Open the inland road. A stamped notice proves Director Voss authorized continuation after a written risk warning. Mara takes the patients to the hospital.
9. **Municipal Records — the suppressed hearing.** Independent originals expose altered consent and missing names. An objection identifies surviving researcher Inez Rook.
10. **Meridian Observatory — the witness.** Reach Rook using a local lamp. She explains that the carrier's biological danger was known before the emergency-voice project. She takes responsibility for her own contribution and consents to evacuation.
11. **Hollow Reservoir — the boundary.** Secure Rook's crossing. A complete survey identifies the one isolated inland test circuit and its demolition vault. The transport ledger connects nine trial victims to Alder House. No unseen relay is added after this boundary.
12. **Alder House — nine people.** Recover names, photographs, preferences and family contacts. They were people before they were combat targets. The caretaker's preserved plan opens the route to the original evidence.
13. **Witness Vault — preserve the truth.** Authenticate the authorization, warning, transport ledger, identities and deposition. A physical courier carries the case outside the valley before machinery destruction becomes possible.
14. **The Quiet Line — return to the living.** Isolate both ends of the documented loop, destroy its stored medium, verify the gap, and leave. Mara opens the daylight gate. A three-part epilogue resolves the outbreak, the public hearing and Alex's return to ordinary life. Voss answers under his own name; Rook testifies in person; the families receive the victims by theirs. Alex can answer a simple request from Mara without fear.

Optional keepsakes acknowledge ordinary lives at the ending without deciding whether the main resolution is valid. Recovering supplies or memorabilia is never required to expose the trial or survive the final narrative.

## Delivery

- A 32-second skippable opening cinematic establishes the call, arrival and first objective in four illustrated shots followed by a title reveal. It uses timed text, camera motion, fades and synthesized sound rather than recorded voice acting. The menu can replay it without changing the current expedition.
- The original three optional records per chapter supply physical evidence, instructions and character context. A fourth service-cache cipher on every floor connects an optional three-latch puzzle to extra evidence and equipment.
- Chapters 5–7 open with two skippable illustrated cards each. They remain text scenes without recorded voice acting or video. The final extraction uses a separate illustration of Mara.
- Chapter completion screens deliver the essential revelations even if records are missed.
- PA captions announce objective activity; they are intentionally unreliable in the fiction. Written records carry the verifiable evidence.
- The fourteen-chapter campaign ends conclusively at The Quiet Line. Relay 6 and Breakwater remain shut down. The inland test circuit is documented in full before the final approach and is physically destroyed; there is no final surprise transmission.

- Thirty-five new personal records and seven keepsakes deepen the world outside the conspiracy. Recovered records persist in the campaign journal. Seven new supply caches retain the existing three-latch cipher mechanic.
- New chapters open with illustrated cards. The final epilogue is three manually advanced, skippable pages; no recorded voice acting.

## Continuity rules

Mara's genuine messages are written or stored on a local recorder. The misleading distress call is ECHO's reconstruction. Alex is a trained station technician, explaining machinery interactions; the pistol comes from the emergency security post. The zombies are established as carrier-exposed personnel, not a separate unrelated outbreak. Keep future expansions consistent with these facts, or explicitly revise this guide and the in-game records together.
